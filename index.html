Our Institution

About Our School

GIC Silari was established in 1989 and is managed by the Department of School Education, Government of Uttarakhand.
Atal Utkrisht Government Inter College, Silari, is a reputed school located in Silari, Pratapnagar, Tehri Garhwal, Uttarakhand. Surrounded by the beautiful natural environment of the region, the school provides a peaceful and inspiring atmosphere for learning.

Our classrooms are spacious, clean, airy, and well-maintained. Dedicated and hardworking teachers guide students with care and encourage them to develop their knowledge, skills, confidence, and values.

The school is affiliated with the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE) and has been providing quality education to students since 2021. We are committed to creating a positive learning environment where every student gets opportunities to learn, grow, and achieve their potential.

🎯

Our Vision

Our vision is to help every student grow into a confident, curious, responsible, and passionate individual.
We aim to provide an environment that encourages students to think creatively, develop strong values, and participate actively in different areas of learning and life.
We are committed to the all-round development of every student and to preparing them to face the future with confidence, compassion, and a positive attitude.

🚀

Our Mission

Our mission is to empower every student with quality education, essential life skills, strong values, and confidence.
We strive to develop students who are hardworking, responsible, well-versed, disciplined, and respectful towards others. Our aim is to connect with every student, understand their individual needs, and provide them with meaningful opportunities to learn and grow.
Through dedicated teaching, practical learning, co-curricular activities, and character development, we encourage our students to become responsible citizens and lifelong learners.

🌱

Our Values

Discipline

We believe that discipline is an essential part of a student's life. We encourage our students to be disciplined not only at school but also in every aspect of their lives. Discipline helps students develop responsibility, self-control, punctuality, and respect for others.

Integrity & Equality

We believe in treating every student with fairness, dignity, and respect. Our school promotes an inclusive environment where students are valued equally, regardless of caste, gender, background, or other differences.

Academic Excellence

Our school provides education from Class VI to Class XII and strives to create a supportive environment for academic growth. Along with knowledge, we encourage students to develop good values, confidence, curiosity, and a positive attitude towards learning.

What We Offer

Why AUGIC Silari

📚

Academics

Our school provides a positive and supportive academic environment where students are encouraged to learn with curiosity and confidence.
Subjects such as English, Mathematics, Science, and other disciplines are taught through clear explanations and practical examples. Our teachers are dedicated to helping students understand concepts effectively and develop a strong foundation of knowledge.

Students also get opportunities to use the Computer Laboratory, Physics Laboratory, Chemistry Laboratory, and Biology Laboratory for practical learning and better understanding of concepts.
Along with academics, students are encouraged to participate in sports and other activities that contribute to their overall development..

🎓

Education System

Atal Utkrisht Government Inter College, Silari, is a co-educational school providing education from Class VI to Class XII.

The school provides an inclusive learning environment where boys and girls learn and grow together. With a focus on academics, discipline, values, practical learning, and co-curricular activities, we aim to support the overall development of every student.
The school currently has 286 students, who are provided opportunities to learn, participate, and develop their skills in a supportive educational environment.
